   ARKS
UNLEASHED

As the Balefleets of Warmaster Abaddon and Vashtorr the
Arkifane tore their bloody paths across the galaxy, world after
world was plunged into cataclysmic conflict. Traitors and
heretics of every stripe did battle with defenders both Imperial
and xenos — and occasionally even heretic — in the quest for
the Key-fragments.


Where the coordinated — if lumbering — Indomitus Crusade
fleets of the Imperium swept through entire sub—sectors at a
time, the Arks of Omen and their Balefleets instead targeted
individual systems and often specific worlds. The traitors brought
concentrated military strength to bear against often—unsuspecting
targets, leading to especially Vicious battles that caused dreadful
damage and slaughter.


Even when some factions across the galaxy began their fightback,
each fresh conflict spawned by the Arks of Omen was focused
and intense. Many took place aboard warring void ships, orbital
defence platforms and even the Arks of Omen themselves.
Funnelled into the claustrophobic confines of ill—lit corridors and
chambers crowded with thrumming machinery, such fights came
down to running gun battles and savage hand—to—hand combat.
Each bulkhead door, choke point or valuable ship’s system became
a vital strategic objective for which rivers of blood were shed and
bodies stacked high. Such was the nightmare war that the Arks of
Omen brought to the galaxy.
